            Passenger Help Center
            
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

          

          Clear answers before you book
        


        

          

            
How do I view or print my Boarding Pass / E-Ticket?

            
Select Find Reservation at the top search box, enter your PNR code (e.g. KTA...) and passenger Last Name to display your boarding pass, barcode, and print/save options.

          

          

            
How does the Larnaca (LCA) + Vista Mare stay option work?

            
When booking a flight to or from Larnaca (LCA), you can attach a Vistamare room type in Yeni İskele, choose nights, and optionally add a private LCA pick-up transfer.

          

          

            
Is my payment information safe on this site?

            
Yes. Card payments run through Tranzilla with card number validation, masked card preview, and zero storage of full card numbers or CVV codes in local browser storage.

          

          

            
How can I request assistance with passenger details or changes?

            
Contact K.T.A support directly by phone at +972 3 510 05 99 or by email at service@ktair.net with your PNR reference.
